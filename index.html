About Herman

Four years on Council, twenty‑five years in the neighbourhood.

During the past 4 years, it has really been an honour and privilege to serve the District as one of your Councillors. Having lived on the North Shore for almost 25 years and served in volunteer and Board roles with many organizations across the lower mainland, I have the experience with governance, business acumen and collaborative approach to continue representing you on Council. I was first elected to District of North Vancouver Council in 2022.

Herman Mah

  • 25years living on the North Shore

Away from Council, Herman enjoys outdoor activities, pickleball, skiing and playing ice hockey (ASHL) at Canlan Sports.

My approach

A balanced and thoughtful way of deciding.

When dealing with issues, I take a balanced and thoughtful approach. This includes listening to input from residents and businesses in our Community, evaluating staff’s professional analyses and bringing experience from my diverse personal and business background. I consult with as many people as possible in arriving at the best decisions for the entire Community.

Priorities

The District is experiencing many changes and challenges. I will continue to help us to move forward while respecting the positive things that we love about living and working in the District. I will work collaboratively with Council, First Nations, senior levels of government and other parties to enhance our quality of life in the District by addressing these key issues.

  1. iTraffic, congestion and infrastructure
  2. iiSupporting and promoting local economy
  3. iiiAffordable housing with a strong sense of community
